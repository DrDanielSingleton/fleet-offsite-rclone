Privacy policy for fleet-offsite-rclone

fleet-offsite-rclone is operated by Daniel for personal use.

The app uses Google authorization to access only the specific Google Drive files used with or created by the app. It uses that access to store and retrieve encrypted backups. Backup data is encrypted locally by restic before it is uploaded to Google Drive.

Google stores the encrypted backup files and receives the account and file metadata needed to provide its service. OAuth credentials are used locally to authorize the backup utility. They are not included in these public pages.

The app does not sell data or use it for advertising. Backup data is retained until Daniel deletes it. Access can be revoked through the Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access does not itself delete existing backup files.

This policy covers the personal backup utility. Google's services are also subject to Google's own privacy policy.
